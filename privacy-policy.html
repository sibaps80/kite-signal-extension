Smart Trader Extension - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Trader ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that automatically places trading orders on your Zerodha Kite broker account based on algorithmic trading signals generated by a connected backend system.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Authentication Data

  • Broker Session Token (Kite): The Extension reads your Kite session token (enctoken) from browser cookies to authenticate order placement requests. This token is never stored on disk or sent to our servers.
  • Broker Client ID: Used to validate your broker account and route orders correctly.

2.2 Trading Data

  • Signals received: Symbol, direction (BUY/SELL), price, timestamp
  • Orders placed: Order ID, quantity, status, timestamps
  • Order confirmations: From your broker after execution

2.3 Configuration Data (Stored Locally)

  • Backend server URL
  • Auto-trade preference (on/off)
  • Paper mode setting
  • Broker preference (Kite/Dhan)
  • Last seen signal timestamp (for deduplication)

2.4 What We DO NOT Collect

  • Your password or login credentials
  • Account number or PAN
  • Account balance or holdings
  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Browser history or other websites visited
  • Keystroke logging or screen recording
🔒 Important: All authentication tokens (Kite's enctoken) exist only in browser memory during your session. They are NEVER stored on disk and are automatically cleared when you close your browser or log out.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Order Placement: Your session token is used ONLY to place trading orders on your behalf via Kite's official API.
  • Signal Delivery: We receive trading signals from your configured backend server and display them in the Extension.
  • Validation: Your broker client ID is validated against our backend to ensure you're an authorized user.
  • Error Handling: Order errors and connection failures are logged for debugging purposes.
  • Preferences: Your settings are stored locally in your browser for convenience.

4. Where Your Data Goes

4.1 Data Storage Locations

  • Browser Local Storage: Configuration and preferences stay on YOUR machine only.
  • Your Broker (Kite/Dhan): Order data is sent directly to your broker's servers.
  • Your Backend Server: Trading signals are received from the backend server you configure (typically dailyfactshub.com).

4.2 Third Parties

We DO NOT share your data with:

  • Analytics companies
  • Advertising networks
  • Data brokers or marketers
  • Other extensions or apps
  • Any third party except your broker and your configured backend

5. Data Retention

  • Session Tokens: Cleared immediately when browser closes or you log out.
  • Configuration: Stored locally until you uninstall the Extension.
  • Signal History: Displayed in the Extension UI but not permanently stored (browser cache only).
  • Order Data: Exists on your broker's servers per their data retention policy.

When you uninstall the Extension: All local data is automatically deleted by Chrome. Nothing persists.

6. Your Rights and Controls

6.1 User Controls

  • Disable Auto-Trade: You can toggle auto-trade on/off anytime. Paper mode is the default for safety.
  • View Local Data: Open Chrome DevTools → Application → Local Storage to see what's stored.
  • Clear Data: Uninstall the Extension to delete all local data.

6.2 No Tracking or Analytics

We do NOT use:

  • Google Analytics or similar tracking
  • Crash reporting with data collection
  • Telemetry or usage statistics
  • Advertising pixels or beacons

7. Security Measures

  • HTTPS Only: All communication with backend and Kite APIs uses HTTPS encryption.
  • No Disk Storage: Authentication tokens never touch your disk.
  • Minimal Permissions: Extension requests only the absolute minimum permissions needed.
  • Browser Sandbox: Extension runs in Chrome's security sandbox with no access to your OS.
  • No Remote Code Execution: Extension code is reviewed by Chrome before publication.

8. Compliance

Smart Trader complies with:

  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies - Honest representation of functionality
  • User Data Policy - Transparent data collection and usage
  • No Deceptive Practices: The Extension clearly states it auto-places orders; user consent is obtained at installation.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Extension or legal requirements. You will be notified of material changes through:

  • Update to this policy on our website
  • Chrome Web Store listing update notification

Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
